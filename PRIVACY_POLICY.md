# Privacy Policy for Kesandu Guru

**Last Updated: February 7, 2026**

## Overview

Kesandu Guru ("the App") is an AI education platform. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle data.

## Data Collection

### Data We Collect
- **Progress Data**: Your XP, completed challenges, and level are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device.
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information.
- **No Analytics**: We do not use third-party analytics or tracking services.
- **No Accounts**: The app does not require account creation or login.

### Data We Do NOT Collect
- Location data
- Contacts or address book
- Photos or camera access
- Microphone access
- Browsing history
- Device identifiers for advertising

## Third-Party Services

### YouTube
The App embeds YouTube videos for educational content. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to video playback. We do not control or have access to data YouTube may collect during video playback.
- YouTube Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device. We do not have servers that store your data. If you uninstall the app, all local data is deleted.

## Data Export and Deletion

You can:
- **Export** your progress data from the Settings screen
- **Delete** all your data by using the "Reset All Progress" option in Settings, or by uninstalling the app

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains educational content suitable for general audiences.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact:
- Email: privacy@kesandu.com

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your data including:
- Right to access your data (available via Export in Settings)
- Right to delete your data (available via Reset in Settings)
- Right to data portability (available via Export in Settings)
